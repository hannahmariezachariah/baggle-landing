What is Baggle

Overwhelmed by your online shopping experience?

Baggle removes the stress from shopping online. Forget the open tabs, forget the pressure to buy right now, simply save anything you want, or might want, to Baggle and come back to it whenever you like.

No more spam filling up your inbox. Just a quiet notification on your Baggle account when the price actually drops.
