Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

This policy explains what Baggle ("Baggle", "we", "us") collects when you use the Baggle browser extension, the Baggle web dashboard, or baggle.net (together, the "Service"), and what we do with it. Baggle is currently pre-launch — we're still finishing the product before any public release — but we wanted this in place now because the Service already stores real account and shopping data for the people trying it.

What we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we store your email address and password (handled by our database provider, Supabase, which never gives us your password in plain text). If you add a name in "Edit profile," we store that too.

Profile picture (optional). If you upload one in "Edit profile," your browser first crops it to the part you chose and removes any camera metadata (such as location), then we store that small image with our storage provider, Supabase. It is saved at a web address that works without logging in, so anyone who has the exact link can open the image; the link is long and unguessable. Your picture is shown to other signed-in Baggle users next to your name (for example on your shared bags, in search, and to the owner of a bag you follow or like), and they can click it to enlarge it. This applies whether your account is public or private. Your picture is not shown on the logged-out baggle.net homepage previews.

Bags and items. When you save something with the Baggle extension, we store the product's title, price, size (if you add one), the store it's from, its image, its page link, and when you saved it — organised into the "bags" you create.

Price and stock history. To send you price-drop and back-in-stock alerts, we periodically re-check the page link of each saved item and store the price and stock status we find.

Social features, if you choose to use them. Baggle lets you make your account and individual bags public, so a friend with their own Baggle account can open a share link, follow a bag, and like it. If you do this, we store: your account's public/private setting, each bag's own private setting, who follows which bag, and who's liked which bag.

Public previews on baggle.net. If you set your account to public, bags you haven't marked private can appear on the baggle.net homepage, which anyone can see without an account. That preview shows only the bag's name, your display name, how many items it holds, how many likes it got this week, a few of its item photos, and how many people follow your public bags. Item titles, prices and store links are only visible to signed-in Baggle users. Set your account or a bag back to private and it stops appearing.

One thing worth calling out specifically: if you follow or like someone else's bag, the owner of that bag can see your name attached to that follow or like — even if your own account is set to private. Your private setting still hides everything else about you (your own bags stay invisible to anyone you haven't shared them with); it just doesn't hide the fact that you followed or liked a bag someone else owns, from that person specifically. Nobody other than that bag's owner gets this.

Finding people. Like most social apps, Baggle lets any signed-in user search for people by name. A search result shows the person's name, their picture if they added one, and whether the account is public or private. If an account is private, nothing else about it is shown: its bags stay hidden. If you would rather not appear in search, you can leave out your name in "Edit profile," but your name is how friends find you.

What we don't collect

The Baggle extension only reads product details from a page when you actively use it to save an item — it doesn't track, log, or send us the pages you browse otherwise. Baggle.net and the web dashboard don't run any analytics or advertising trackers today.

How we use your data

  • To run the Service: storing your bags, checking prices, and sending you alerts.
  • To provide the sharing, following, and liking features you choose to turn on.
  • To send account-related emails, like confirming your email address or resetting your password.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Affiliate links

Baggle has the technical groundwork in place to route "Go to store" links through an affiliate network (such as Skimlinks or Sovrn Commerce), which could earn Baggle a small commission on purchases made that way at no extra cost to you. This isn't switched on yet. If and when it is, clicking through to a store may pass the destination link through that network before you arrive — the product link we've stored for you doesn't change either way, and we'll update this policy when it goes live.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of outside services to run Baggle, each only for the purpose described:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and the automated job that checks prices. This is where all the data described above actually lives.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts baggle.net and the web dashboard as static files.
  • Google Fonts — loads the typeface used on this site; your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch it.

We don't share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

Security

Your data is protected by row-level security rules enforced directly by our database, not just by the app's own code — meaning each person can only ever read or change the rows the rules explicitly allow, whether that's their own data or something another user has deliberately made visible to them (like a public, followed bag). Baggle only ever ships a restricted, public API key in the extension and dashboard; nothing with broader access ever leaves our servers.

Your choices

  • You can make your account or any individual bag private again at any time.
  • You can unfollow or unlike a bag at any time.
  • You can change your display name at any time.
  • You can change or remove your profile picture at any time, which deletes the stored image.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the dashboard, which deletes your bags, items, and social activity along with it.

Children's privacy

Baggle isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at privacy@baggle.net.
